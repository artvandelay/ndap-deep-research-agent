Depth — how hard it digs
Fast gives a quick grounded answer from the top matching datasets.
Deep reads more datasets and reflects across rounds for harder, multi-part questions.
Personality — how it writes
Objective is concise, sober, answer-first writing.
Judgemental makes sharper, evidence-grounded calls on patterns, correlations, and gaps — the numbers stay NDAP-only.

Settings

Stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent directly to OpenRouter from your device. Never uploaded anywhere else.

Workhorse tier: search, planning, dataset selection, and the browser-local tool loop. Cheap/fast models are fine here, e.g. openai/gpt-5.4-nano.

Synthesis tier: always writes the final answer, regardless of depth or personality. Use a stronger model, e.g. anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6.

Enables real numbers: the agent downloads dataset rows through this CORS proxy and computes the answer. Pre-filled with a shared demo proxy; clear it for discovery-only mode, or deploy your own (see proxy/ in the repo).

How many candidate datasets the index search surfaces for the model to choose from (applies to both Fast and Deep depth). Higher = broader recall, more tokens.

In Deep depth the agent downloads and analyzes up to this many datasets (real-numbers mode), reflecting between rounds to fill gaps. Higher = more thorough but slower and costlier.
